## The One Thing That Determines Whether This Works

You asked what level of prep is required. The honest answer is not a number of problems or hours. It is this:

**The level required is the level where you are no longer surprised by problems — where you have seen enough patterns that every new problem feels like a variation of something you understand, not something entirely foreign.**

You described exactly the opposite right now — solving Striver A2Z but still being surprised by unfamiliar graph problems. That gap closes through one thing only: **independent solving under time pressure, followed by honest reflection on what clue you missed.**

The 3-Stage Protocol from the previous plan is not a technique. It is the entire mechanism by which the gap you described closes. Every problem you solve by watching a video first is a problem that didn't contribute to closing that gap. Every problem where you struggled for 12 minutes, got stuck, then discovered the insight — that one moved the needle.

Nine months, done this way, genuinely transforms problem-solving ability. Nine months of watching solutions and hitting problem quotas does not.

The prep level required is not Hard. It is **Honest**.
